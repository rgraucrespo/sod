Citing SOD
==========

If you use SOD in your research, please cite the original SOD paper, which
describes the underlying methodology:

   Grau-Crespo, R., Hamad, S., Catlow, C. R. A., & de Leeuw, N. H. (2007).
   *Symmetry-adapted configurational modelling of fractional site occupancy in
   solids.* **Journal of Physics: Condensed Matter**, 19(25), 256201.
   `doi:10.1088/0953-8984/19/25/256201
   <https://doi.org/10.1088/0953-8984/19/25/256201>`_

Method-specific references
--------------------------

If you use the SQS or GQS functionality (:doc:`sqs_gqs`), please also cite:

   Hamad, S., Catlow, C. R. A., Spanó, E., Deutschmann, O., & Grau-Crespo, R.
   (2022). *Special Quasirandom Structures for the calculation of properties of
   solid solutions at arbitrary temperatures: the case of complex
   borocarbides.* **Chemistry of Materials**, 34(9), 3988–3995.

Applications of the method that may be useful as worked references are listed
with the corresponding worked examples in :doc:`examples`.
